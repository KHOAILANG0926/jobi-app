Original Vietnam provincial polygons and label points from Natural Earth ADM1,
as distributed in markmarkoh/datamaps src/js/data/vnm.json, pinned repository
commit 14c1641273bb52e6f115f99db847ec076c62eb4b (retrieved 2026-10-28).
https://github.com/markmarkoh/datamaps/blob/14c1641273bb52e6f115f99db847ec076c62eb4b/src/js/data/vnm.json
Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
These are generalized original pre-2025 province polygons, not current ward
boundaries, cadastral data or workplace evidence. Unknown city boundaries
must be reported as unavailable. No merger alias maps one original province
to the center of another. Canonical coordinates remain job_work_locations.
